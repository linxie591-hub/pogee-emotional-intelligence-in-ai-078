Emotional Intelligence in AI

Reading the room is harder than answering the question.

Emotional intelligence in a companion means noticing a shift in tone, matching energy, and knowing when to be quiet rather than clever.

It depends on memory: you cannot respond to how someone has changed if you never recorded where they started.

Empathy is a memory problem before it is a language problem.
